🎓 Technical Curriculum (What to Master)

📊 Data Representation Types

  • Line Graphs & Time Series: Identifying trends across periods, points of inflection.
  • Bar Charts: Simple, Stacked, and Grouped representations comparing multiple dimensions.
  • Pie Charts: Single & Dual Pie charts, Angle-to-Percentage conversion ($\text{Value} = \frac{\theta}{360} \times \text{Total}$).
  • Tabular Data: Reading complex multi-column matrix tables accurately without misalignment.
  • Mixed Diagrams: Combos of Pie Chart + Table, or Line Graph + Bar Chart.

🧮 Mathematical Core Concepts

  • Percentages: Net change, Percentage Increase/Decrease ($\frac{\text{Diff}}{\text{Original}} \times 100$).
  • Ratio & Proportion: Share division, standard simplifications, cross-comparisons.
  • Averages: Simple averages and Weighted averages ($\frac{\sum w_i x_i}{\sum w_i}$).
  • Business Math: Profit & loss percentages, cost price, selling price, and profit margins.
  • Growth Rates: CAGR vs. Simple annual growth rate (SARG) analysis.
