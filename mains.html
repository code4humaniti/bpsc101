Why are GS-2 Marks Comparatively Low? (GS-1 vs GS-2 Analysis)

GS Paper 1 (High Scoring Potential)

  • Contains a 72-mark Statistics/DI section where candidates can easily score a perfect 72/72.
  • History topics (Maurya Art, Pala Art, Patna Qalam, Tribal uprisings) are highly repetitive and predictable.
  • High scores usually cluster around 180-195 marks.

GS Paper 2 (Strict Evaluation)

  • Entirely subjective paper containing Indian Polity, Economy/Geography of Bihar, and Science & Tech.
  • Polity and Economic responses are evaluated strictly, requiring critical analysis, articles, and recommendations (Punchhi, Sarkaria).
  • Top scores rarely exceed 155-162 marks. Even a score of 140+ is considered excellent.
