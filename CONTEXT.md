# BPSC Preparation & Analytics Context

This context defines the ubiquitous language for the BPSC Minimalist 2-Page Preparation Portal, covering exam stages, score benchmarks, focused single-source resources, and bilingual Devanagari Hindi & English accessibility.

## 2-Page Architecture

**Prelims Command Center (`index.html`)**:
The minimalist command center for the 150-mark objective BPSC Prelims screening stage:
- Top Center Switch: `[ 📍 Prelims | 📜 Mains ]`
- Exam Pattern & Mechanics: 150 Qs, 4 Options (A-D), 1/3rd Negative Marking (-0.33)
- Category-Wise Historical Prelims Cutoffs Table (66th to 71st CCE: UR Male/Female, EWS Male/Female, BC, EBC, SC, ST)
- Syllabus & Subject Weightage Breakdown
- Ultra-Focused Prelims Resource Stack (Spectrum, Class 9-10/11-12 NCERTs, Ghatna Chakra Purvavalokan, Imtiaz Ahmad, Eduteria)
- Safe-Score & 1/3rd Negative Penalty Calculator

**Mains Command Center (`mains.html`)**:
The minimalist command center for the 1020-mark BPSC Mains written stage and Interview:
- Top Center Switch: `[ 📍 Prelims | 📜 Mains ]`
- Exam Structure & Score Mechanics: 1020 Total Merit Marks (GS 1: 300, GS 2: 300, Essay: 300, Interview: 120) + Qualifying Hindi (100) & MCQ Optional (100)
- Category-Wise Final Selection Cutoffs Table (68th to 70th CCE - Out of 1020)
- GS 1, GS 2 & Essay Detailed Syllabus Matrix (Highlighting 72 marks Statistics in GS 1)
- Ultra-Focused Mains Resource Stack (Spectrum, Imtiaz Ahmad, Laxmikanth, Bihar Economic Survey & Budget, Applied S&T Notes)
- Devanagari Bihar Proverbs Library & Section 3 Essay Strategy

## Resource Curation Philosophy

**Single-Source Focus**:
Restricting each subject to 1 primary textbook/notes + 1 practice tool to prevent resource fatigue and ensure candidates score >80% with high efficiency.

**Native Bilingual View**:
Side-by-side presentation of text in both Devanagari Hindi and English across all tables, syllabi, resources, and proverbs.
